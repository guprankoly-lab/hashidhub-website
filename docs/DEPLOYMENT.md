# HashidHub 2027 Roadmap

## Vision
Transform HashidHub into a global social and creator platform combining social discovery, short-form video, creator monetization, business networking, professional growth, and community commerce.

## Phase 1 — Foundation and Product Architecture (Q1 2027)
- Finalize product domain model and user personas.
- Split frontend into feature modules: social feed, reels, communities, jobs, marketplace, academy.
- Add world-class Firestore schema and indexing strategy.
- Set up global verification and moderation workflows.
- Implement language framework for Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Turkish, and German.

## Phase 2 — AI and Discovery (Q2 2027)
- Deploy Hashid AI assistant for writing, translation, and recommendations.
- Add AI-powered post creation, comment suggestions, and translation layer.
- Introduce global search across users, posts, reels, pages, and groups.
- Add personalized discovery engine and recommendation scoring.

## Phase 3 — Creator Economy (Q3 2027)
- Launch Creator Center with analytics, growth metrics, and earnings projections.
- Add creator payout infrastructure, creator fund, and revenue dashboards.
- Enable marketplace listing creation and direct monetization.
- Build verification hierarchy for creators, businesses, and organizations.

## Phase 4 — Professional and Commerce Layers (Q4 2027)
- Launch jobs and hiring infrastructure.
- Add business profiles, service marketplaces, and offer listings.
- Launch academy with course catalog, certificates, and learning paths.
- Improve profile portfolio, skills, achievements, and highlights.

## Phase 5 — Engagement and Retention (Q1 2028)
- Expand gamification: XP, levels, badges, streaks, challenges.
- Launch community quests, creator missions, and achievement unlock flow.
- Introduce engagement loops for daily streaks, milestones, and rewards.

## Phase 6 — Global Scale and Governance (Q2 2028)
- Add regional content moderation and trust workflows.
- Improve global infrastructure, CDN, and media orchestration.
- Add AI-assisted moderation, abuse detection, and scam reduction.
- Build analytics and experimentation platform for content and monetization decisions.

## Strategic Product Pillars

### 1. Social Discovery
- Shared feed, community groups, pages, and networking.

### 2. Short-Form Video
- Reels and creator-led discovery.

### 3. Professional Network
- Jobs, business profiles, skill proof, collaboration.

### 4. Creator Economy
- Earnings, fund programs, marketplace commerce, and supporter communities.

### 5. Learning and Growth
- Academy, certification, and tutorials.

### 6. Global Trust and Safety
- Verification, moderation, and anti-abuse systems.

## Key Milestones
- M1: AI + Search release
- M2: Creator Center + Marketplace
- M3: Jobs + Academy
- M4: XP + Verifications + Global localization
- M5: Scale + moderation + payouts

## Success Metrics
- Active creators per month
- Daily engagement rate
- New verified businesses and creators
- Marketplace GMV and conversion
- Search adoption and CTR
- Course completion and certification conversion
- Creator earnings and retention

## Technical Planning Principles
- Frontend modules, not giant monoliths
- Firebase Firestore collections with strong indexes
- Stateless serverless functions for sensitive actions
- Observability and event tracing from day one
- Content safety controls before global launch
